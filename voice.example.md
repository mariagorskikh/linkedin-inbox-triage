## Style rules

- Length: <e.g. 1 to 2 sentences, never more than 3>
- Greeting: <e.g. "Hi First!" by default, "Hey First!" with people I know well>
- Exclamation points: <e.g. yes, "!!" for congratulations>
- Emoji: <e.g. at most one ":)" at the end, nothing else>
- Sign-off: <e.g. none>
- Slow replies: <e.g. open with "so sorry for the slow reply!" if older than 3 days>
- Never say: <e.g. "circle back", "synergy", "hope this finds you well">
